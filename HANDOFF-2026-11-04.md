## 1. What's here, by family

The Vault's pages fall into three families. Each family shares one look; the families differ on purpose.

| Family | Pages | Shape | Plan doc |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Manage and share** | People, Documents | `WorkspacePage` (a template): a list beside a record in a framed panel | `VIEWS-PLAN.md` |
| **Keep and compose** | Passwords, Memories, Messages | One reading column (`Page width="focused"`): list → item → form | `COLLECTIONS-PLAN.md` |
| **Guided steps** | Will Builder, and the will page | `SetupBeat` (Essential Setup's template): one question per step | `WILL-PLAN.md` |

Each plan doc has the intent (from the Vue pages, their specs, the API and the ADRs), the UI decided, what `@paige/ui` had, the open questions, and a dated **Progress** section with every decision George made. **Read those docs first**; this file is the map.

### Manage and share: People, Documents (`labs/vault/views`, `labs/vault/people`, `labs/vault/documents`)
- **`WorkspacePage`** (`src/templates`): the band, then a framed panel at `border-medium`: a white list, and a stage in the page tint. The stage bar and the list's search row are both 72 (`--spacing-workspace-bar`), so their rules meet. On a phone the back arrow sits in the stage bar.
- **Records** (`labs/vault/record-section.tsx`):
  - RecordCard and RecordSection: heading-level section names; the **type and spacing rules** are in the file's header.
  - AreaLines.
  - Letterheads: the person's, and the document's (with a file-type tile).
- **Documents:**
  - **Needs you** is the queue, and the only place values are acted on: Looks right, Fix, Use this / Keep mine, and **Confirm all**. Settled values move to **What we read**.
  - Download, Share and ⋯ are in the stage bar.
  - Who it names and Who it's shared with show avatars.
  - Empty sections are hidden.
- **Hand-over:** Needs you is going to its own session. See `lane-6-needs-you-handoff.md` in the shared notes folder.

### Keep and compose: Passwords, Memories, Messages (`labs/vault/collections`)
- **Shared:** `FindRow` (`labs/patterns`: search, the caller's tools, one filter set as Chips) and `useRoutes` (hrefs as routes in labs; in the app the router does it).
- **Passwords:** grouped by type, plus the next-kind prompt. An account shows Signing in, where each secret is a **SecretValue** that asks for it when shown. Add and edit use **RepeatField** for credentials and **FileField** for files.
- **Memories:**
  - The list has **Grid · Timeline** (our icon ToggleGroup). The timeline is **AlternatingTimeline** (`labs/patterns`). The cards are **MemoryCard**, with the picture leading.
  - A memory shows its picture and its story in parts.
  - The form uses **SectionsField** (the category's prompts as suggestions, ↑ ↓) and one file.
- **Messages:** grouped by when they go. A letter shows when it goes, with "Paige doesn't send yet", then the rich letter and its files. The form uses **DeliveryRuleField** (only the chosen choice's inputs) and up to 2 files.

### Guided steps: Will Builder (`labs/vault/will`)
- **WillBuilder:**
  - **The steps:** You · Spouse (married only) · Children and guardians · Who carries it out · Who inherits · Gifts · Left out · Review.
  - **Who inherits** is one step: a choice that fits the family, then **ShareField** (must total 100).
  - **People are picked from the Vault** with **PersonSlots**.
  - **Review** has an Edit per answer, then Finalize, or Pay and finalize.
  - **Finalize and sign:** finalize first, then download the clean copy, then the state's checklist. It ends with "Open my will" (the document in Documents).
- **WillHub:** the will itself as one card. Not started shows the outline; a draft shows progress and Continue; finalized shows Open it, Download and Update, then what it says and how to sign. Earlier versions appear only when there's more than one.

## 4. Open questions (all in the plan docs' §4)

- **Security (George):** the Passwords get returns every secret in plaintext; there's no reveal-on-demand, re-auth or audit, and delete is hard. The labs data assumes secrets are fetched one at a time when asked.
- **API contract:**
  - Documents: whether each value's state, read date and Area come back.
  - The Messages body: the editor's JSON vs the wire's HTML.
  - Message recipients aren't on the wire.
  - The memory categories and message types are stand-ins.
  - Will: server-side validation (executors, the 100% total), writing `currentSection`, a checkout tied to the draft, and filling the spouse and children from the Vault.
- **Product:** every word marked **⟨new⟩** in the fixtures needs Product, including "Ready" for Scheduled, "What we read", and "In your Vault". The spouse's will is half-built: hide it until it's real.
- **Later (George):** more Memories views and better filters; Needs you; staggering the timeline; the "Change it in Personal too?" prompt; Essential Setup skipping read approval.
