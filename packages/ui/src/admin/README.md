# Admin — the admin site's pieces

Moved from `packages/ui/CLAUDE.md` (R17-14): the decisions behind the admin pieces in this folder. The package rules and layers are in `../../CLAUDE.md`.

## Admin (R8-06…R8-11)

The admin site's pieces follow the same layers (ORGANIZATION.md § Admin readiness has the verdict). Admin pages are desktop-first ("better, not perfect", George): AdminPage uses the inset's full width; it works on a phone (the sidebar becomes a sheet) but isn't designed for one.

**Two folders** (George 2026-10-30). The root holds the parts the site is built from (AdminSidebar and `adminNav`, MetricCard, PipelineGraph, PartnerLogo, PartnerOffers, RevShare, the rules files); `pages/` holds one module per admin page: the data its ListPage takes (`peoplePage`, `partnersPage`, `plansPage`, `subscriptionsPage`, `promotionCodesPage`: noun, segments, filters, columns, actions) and the page's own dialogs and sections, plus Pulse's story. In Storybook they are Admin and Admin › Pages. A page imports the root; the root never imports a page: `check:layers` GROUP (the vault's rule) covers `admin/` too, after the move found `partner-offers.tsx` taking `PartnerLogo` from `pages/partners.tsx`; the logo is a part and now lives at the root (`partner-logo.tsx`, Media).

- **A metric card is number + sparkline + one delta, all over the same window** (George). If a metric needs detail to explain it, it's the wrong number: Stat has no footnote, badge or second-number slot. **One caption line is allowed** (R21-05, George 2026-10-31): a muted line under the card saying what the number counts ("people saw Welcome", "14% of those who started"), for summary and funnel cards. **Still no second number**: the Journeys mock's "14 · 14%" is 14 with the share in the caption, never a figure beside the value. KpiCard and Stat take `caption`; MetricCard has none (a headline metric's label and window say what it is). The window is said once, under the label, so the delta shows only the number and the comparison on one line (R11-07: a row of cards stays even); the full sentence ("Up 18% compared with the previous 12 months", `describeDelta` in `lib/metrics.ts`) is what screen readers hear and the delta's Tooltip shows. **Flow or level decides the words** (R12-05, GAPS #124; `comparisonShort`/`comparisonPhrase` in `lib/metrics.ts`, tested): a flow (a total over the window) says "vs prior" and "compared with the previous 12 months"; a level (a count at a moment) says when its comparison point was, "vs a year ago" for "Last 12 months", "vs 30 days ago", or the comparison date (`since`) for any other window. Stat's delta takes `basis` (default flow); MetricCard passes its `kind` and the first point's period, so no caller writes the words. "Over 12 months" was rejected: it reads as growth across the window. MetricCard (`admin/`) derives the number and the delta from the one series it's given (`level`: last point vs first; `flow`: the window's total vs `previousTotal`), so the three can't disagree.
- **Numbers** come from `lib/metrics.ts` (tested): counts compact from 10,000 (12.4K), currency from integer minor units ($16.3K, exact value as the hover title), percent, a quantity with a unit; no value is "—", never 0. **Deltas** are percent, net (the metric's format) or points; the arrow follows the sign, the colour follows `good` (up · down · neither): better is success ink, worse is attention ink (not red: red is for irreversible actions, R2-03), neutral is muted; screen readers hear "better"/"worse".
- **Sparkline** is inline SVG (never Recharts): no axes, the last point marked (a running period dashed or outlined), one accessible name summarising the trend (`describeSeries`), hover titles per point.
- **Charts load lazily.** Pages import `@paige/ui/charts/lazy-chart`; `charts/chart.tsx` (and Recharts, ~366 KB minified) arrives only when a chart renders. Customer routes never import `charts/`: on the Next build, Recharts is in one chunk that only `/admin`'s manifest reaches, through a dynamic import (R8-06 check). Series colours are `--chart-1…5`; a hidden table carries the data for screen readers.
- **DataTable**: columns as data (`{ id, header, cell, sortValue?, numeric? }`), numeric columns right-aligned with tabular figures, sticky header inside its box, Columns menu, client pagination, loading/empty/error-with-retry in place. TanStack types never reach a screen. `maxHeight="fill"` (R12-07) makes the table a whole pane: the parent's height, no border of its own, the body scrolling under the sticky header. Its header is its own (R10-09): `title` (a heading, `headingLevel` 2 or 3), `description` and `actions` sit in one toolbar row with the screen's `toolbar` controls and the Columns menu, on the title's baseline; a page doesn't build a heading above the table. R12-10 adds, all optional: `selection` (a checkbox column, the header box for the page, mixed when some), `sorting` and `paging` controlled (the rows arrive sorted and as one page; a numeric column sorts descending first), `onRowClick` (not from a control, a portal or a text selection), `rowActions` (a last column), `emptyState`, `columnsMenu={false}`. The header's line (R13-12) is drawn once by the header cells (`header-rule`, an inset shadow), so it stays under a sticky header while the body scrolls and there is no second line on the first row.
- **The admin home's KPI rows** (R12-08, George): row 1 is three `lg` cards (a 40px number, a 48px sparkline, the window line), row 2 five `compact` cards (a 24px number, the delta, a 24px sparkline, no window line: the row's `caption` says the window once, screen readers still hear it per card). `KpiCard size` md (default) · lg · compact is Stat's `size` plus the card's padding; MetricCard passes it through. `KpiRow` (`patterns/`) lays them out: `columns={3}` one column, three from md; `columns={5}` two, three from lg, five from xl; a labelled region. Designed at 1280 and 1440. Which cards a person sees is chosen later (noted, not built). Grid takes up to 5 columns for it.
- **Every admin page has the admin nav** (R12-09): AdminPage renders AdminSidebar itself from `current` (plus `navBadges`, `linkAs`); there is no `sidebar` prop to forget or replace, so the footer Collapse is always there. Admin page stories render in `src/stories/admin-frame.tsx` (the viewport's height: the fixed, viewport-tall sidebar's footer was cut off below a shorter story frame) and check it with `expectCollapseInView`.
- **No global success banners** (George): completions show in place; AdminPage has no banner slot.
- **The list page pattern** (R12-10, rationale in REVIEW-12; People is the first): `ListPage` (`templates/`) inside AdminPage is filter cards (`FilterCardRow`: KPI-styled segments with counts, one chosen, a radio group; five at most, the compact KPI row's grid) → the toolbar (`ListToolbar`: 200ms-debounced search, the Filters popover, the status line, the result count; R13-06: `FiltersPopover` has one row per filter, a MultiSelect for any-of options, a Select for one, a DateRangeField for a date (`kind: "date-range"`, held in the state and the URL as one `from..to` string), edited as a draft and applied with Apply, emptied with Reset) → the applied filters as `FilterChips` on their own row under the toolbar, with Clear all at its end (R13-04: absent while no filter is set; kept while the bulk bar takes the toolbar's row) → DataTable (checkbox column, row click, the `⋯` `RowActions` column), the toolbar row becoming `BulkActionBar` while rows are selected. State and derivation are `lib/list-query.ts` (tested): segment AND search AND filters, each card counting under the same search and filters, sort, page (reset on any change to what matches); the URL carries all but the search (PII, product rule 7; the app keeps it per tab in sessionStorage, `apps/web/features/admin/use-list-url-state.ts`). Selection survives paging and sorting, clears on any change to what matches and after a bulk action; select-all-matching from the bar. Actions are one typed `ListAction` (link · action · destructive) for rows and selections: destructive last and always confirmed (`ConfirmDialog`, Components › Overlays, a reason for the audit trail), red only if `irreversible` (R2-03), an action that can't apply shown off with its reason. A page supplies data only (noun, segments, filters, search match, columns, actions); `admin/people.tsx` is People's, `admin/partners.tsx` Partners' (R13-22: a row's action may open the page's own dialog instead of a confirm, as Update logo and Manage offers do). A row action may be a submenu (`kind: "menu"`, R15-07: Copy signup link ▸ one item per plan); BulkActionBar leaves submenus out. DataTable's controlled `sorting`/`paging` are the seam for a server-backed list (GAPS #133).
- **Sidebar** (Components › Navigation) is data-driven; the admin's destinations are `adminNav` in `admin/admin-nav.ts` (R9-03): one typed definition the sidebar renders and the app's routes take their paths from (`adminHref("jobs")`); `admin-nav.test.ts` fails when an `apps/web/app/admin` route has no entry. The hrefs stay provisional until the admin route map (GAPS #108). The active item uses the `--sidebar-*` roles (dark blue ink on the blue tint, 4.5:1). The collapse control is in the sidebar's footer (R10-06: `collapseControl`, icon + "Collapse", the icon alone with an "Expand" tooltip in the rail, hidden on a phone), Ctrl/⌘+B still toggles, and AdminPage's top bar has the toggle only on a phone (it opens the sheet). The admin sidebar has no rail (R10-07: `rail={false}`, a plain edge with the default cursor, nothing resizes); resizing is for drawers later.
- **Surfaces** (R9-01): admin content sits on `--surface-page` (`#f7f9fd`, `bg-surface-page`: the step between white and `--surface-muted`, same 220° blue-white hue); cards, tables, charts and graphs stay white on it; the sidebar is white with its 1px border. Customer pages stay on white.
- **DAGs** (R9-06, R10, R11): **DagFrame** (GraphFrame until R11-03: the DAG, steps and their dependencies; the names `Graph`/`GraphFrame` stay free for other kinds of graph, and user journeys get their own component, R10-05) is the frame and its behaviour; **the graph** is the canvas inside it (R10-02). `LazyDagFrame` (pages) / `DagFrame` in `charts/`, React Flow (`@xyflow/react` 12.12.0, MIT) for the viewport and dagre (`@dagrejs/dagre` 3.1.1, MIT) for the layout, both pinned. The frame contract is REVIEW-9 § C, summarised in DagFrame's header comment and its docs page: fit until you interact, zoom 25–200% on Ctrl/⌘ + wheel or pinch only (a plain wheel scrolls the page), our controls, one tab stop with Tab in dependency order, deterministic layout that never re-runs for a status, a details panel with a controlled selection (the page keeps it in the URL), loading/empty/error, a legend, a list view and a text summary. The pure model (layout, order, paths, summary) is `charts/internal/dag-model.ts`, tested under node. React Flow's attribution stays visible, muted (its maintainers ask organisations that hide it to subscribe to Pro). Round 11 (R11-01, correcting R10-01): the header is the title (a heading, `title` or the `label`; `headingLevel`) with Graph/List (the TabsPill, R11-02: `aria-pressed` buttons wearing `tabsPillTriggerVariants` in a `TabsPillList as="div" role="group"`) and full screen, nothing else; below the canvas, the legend row, then the summary row (the live region). `layout` is `"right"` (default), `"down"` or `"down-alternating"` (R10-02: top to bottom, each rank alternately left and right of a centre line, a rank's nodes stacked on its side, every edge leaving and arriving on the inner side and running down the centre channel; for mostly linear runs). One `viewAdjusted` state (R10-03, `data-view-adjusted` on the canvas): a pan or zoom sets it, Fit, `0` or Reset clears it; while clear the graph refits on any size change (a ResizeObserver on the canvas: the panel opening or closing, full screen, the window), while set the view stays and only the selected node is brought into view. When the whole graph can't fit even at 25% (R10-14) the fit shows the selected node and its neighbours (or the first ranks) and a note offers the list view. The status marks are StatusMark (`components/display/status-mark.tsx`, R10-12), the one vocabulary StatusDot draws too; `charts/dag-status.tsx` maps the graph's six statuses onto it (queued → waiting, running → working, succeeded → done, failed → attention, skipped, canceled) and holds DagLegend, without React Flow. **Round 12 design pass** (R12-07, rationale in REVIEW-12): `height="auto"` (default) gives the canvas the graph's proportions (its height at 75% plus the fit padding, 384–560 in 32px steps; it may grow with a live run, never shrink), computed by `charts/internal/dag-shape.ts` (the vocabulary, ranks and height without dagre, so `dag-status.tsx` and LazyDagFrame's same-size skeleton stay out of the lazy chunk); the footer is one row whose counts wear their status marks (the counts are the legend, only the statuses present; still the live region); the controls are one vertical pill bottom left; the minimap shows only while part of the graph is out of view (partial fit, pan or zoom), bottom right, 160 × 120; the details panel is 320, 384 from a 1024 frame (`@container/dag`); loading and empty keep the canvas's height; the list view is the pane itself (DataTable `maxHeight="fill"`, no Columns menu, every row, scrolling under its sticky header), so switching views never changes the height.
- **Journeys** (R21-14, REVIEW-21): one journey's screens — where people stop and how long they stay — on AdminPage with the R21-02 header slots (the trail Admin › Usage, the journey tabs as TabsLine, the source line as `meta`, "Open in Mixpanel" a link out in a new tab). `pages/journeys.tsx` is the page's parts: `JourneysToolbar` (the dates as a labelled Select, how they came in as ToggleGroup `segment`) and `JourneysView` (NavList of screens with counts and a named attention dot on the biggest drop; beside it either the summary — two captioned KpiCards and an InsightCard that opens the biggest drop — and one DataTable of the three screens where people stop most, MeterBar / Formatted / RangeBar cells with a Legend, "Show all", or one screen's card: the trio, a ShareBar of what they did next, "See who stopped here"). Every number comes from `journey-rules.ts` (went on = the next *required* screen's people over this one's; an optional screen is skipped; low is under 75%). Durations are the package's (`formatDuration`), not the mock's "1m 43s". The data is `fixtures/journeys.ts`, the mock's real local Setup numbers.
- **Not in this round:** Sankey (R8-12), and for graphs: collapsing groups, editing, export to image, virtualisation (R9-08 deferred). A whole-region empty or error state (Primer's Blankslate) is deferred too (R10-04, ORGANIZATION.md § 8); DagFrame's and DataTable's own states stand until then.
