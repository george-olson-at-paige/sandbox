- **"is our combobox the same thing as autocomplete?"** Yes (R20-09): ours is the text-field kind, which ReUI names *autocomplete*; their *combobox* is a button that opens a searchable list — our Select/MultiSelect's job. No new part; a line in the guide.

- **"maybe that's because it's radio?"** — partly (R20-03): Radix keeps the items at `tabindex=-1` and forwards from the group, which works with Tab; the ring itself is right (measured). A click never rings. The Focus story just never drew a radio, so there was nothing to compare against.
- **"i also see that this might be a button group with icons?"** — the view switch is a toggle group (one stays on); the +/−/fit/reset column is a button group (R20-11, R20-13).
- **"this is actually a better style for our tables… but this is the standard"** (Rocket's grid) — taken as the *look* target (R20-22, R20-25); ReUI's grid is the *feature* reference; ours keeps the engine.
