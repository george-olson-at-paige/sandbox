**Model (aligned with George 2026-10-20 on the substance; the wording here is ours, not his):**
- **Plans:** a partner's sponsorships, one per segment: **Customer** (no segment), **VIP**, **Employee** (`Sponsorship.Segment` is an open vocabulary). Each plan has its own signup slug, price and launch date.
- **Offers:** the in-app ad strip (`PartnerOffer`). Nothing else is called an offer.
- **Rev share:** a pricing agreement with a partner. Its terms live in HubSpot, which isn't integrated yet. For this checkpoint, the simplest version:
  - **Parameters:** a single set: rate (%), the paid month subscriptions start counting from (default 4th), will purchases counted per person (default 2), refunds deducted (default yes).
  - **Defaults or override:** a partner uses the global defaults or its own override of the whole set.
  - **The rate is entered by hand**, with a note that it isn't connected to HubSpot. A rate is needed to calculate the share; until the integration exists, someone types it in.
  - **Enabled per plan.** A partner has rev share when any of its plans has it enabled. This replaces the reporting code's hard-coded VIP exclusion.
  - **Results are calculated:** eligible people, qualifying revenue, and the share (qualifying revenue × rate). The members' "Revshareable" column in reporting is one of these results.
- **Partner, never bank** when the word means a partner, in the new UI's words and new API names (the reporting code still says bank in places). Naming the kinds of partner is fine: "The banks, credit unions and universities that…".
- **Counts, not prices, in list cells:** one name must not mean different values in different places.
